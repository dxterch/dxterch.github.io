Home turf

Born in Oviedo, Asturias, in northern Spain — a region he's stayed closely tied to throughout his career, including funding a kart circuit for young local drivers.

Endurance side quest

Won the 24 Hours of Le Mans twice (2018, 2019) and a World Endurance Championship title with Toyota, squeezed in during a gap between F1 seats.

Triple Crown chase

Entered the Indianapolis 500 in 2017, part of a long-shot bid at motorsport's unofficial Triple Crown alongside Monaco and Le Mans.

Lifestyle brand

Runs Kimoa, a beachwear and lifestyle label he founded, extending his identity well beyond the cockpit.
